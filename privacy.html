Privacy Policy

SPM Database, effective November 3, 2026

What this app is

SPM Database is a private, internal application built by and for Sports Paradise. It is used only by Sports Paradise employees and is not offered to the public or to other businesses.

What data it accesses

The app connects to Sports Paradise's own QuickBooks Online company with read-only access. It reads accounting records such as bills, bill payments, vendor credits and invoices. It never creates, changes or deletes anything in QuickBooks.

How the data is used

QuickBooks data is used only for Sports Paradise's internal business purposes, such as checking vendor invoices against work orders, tracking vendor claims and internal reporting.

Sharing

QuickBooks data accessed through the app is shown only to Sports Paradise employees. It is not sold, rented or shared with any outside party.

Storage and security

The app runs on a Sports Paradise computer on the company's internal network. QuickBooks connection credentials are stored encrypted and are never placed in source code or public repositories. The connection can be revoked at any time from within the app or from QuickBooks Online.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to loganm@sportsparadise.net.
