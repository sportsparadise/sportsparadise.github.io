SPM Database

Internal tool for Sports Paradise

SPM Database is a private, internal application used only by Sports Paradise employees. It is not available to the public and is not offered to other businesses.

The app connects to Sports Paradise's own QuickBooks Online company with read-only access, to support internal work such as checking vendor invoices against work orders and tracking vendor claims.

Employees open the app from inside the Sports Paradise office network.
